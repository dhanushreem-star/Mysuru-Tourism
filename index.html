About Mysuru

The City of Palaces

A city of culture, heritage and tradition

Mysuru is one of the most popular tourist destinations in Karnataka. It is famous for its magnificent palaces, beautiful gardens, historic monuments and rich cultural heritage.

Mysuru was historically associated with the Wadiyar dynasty and played an important role in the history and culture of the region.

The city is also famous for the grand Mysuru Dasara celebrations, attracting visitors from different parts of India and the world.

Tourist Attractions

Famous Places to Visit in Mysuru

Explore some of the most beautiful and popular attractions in and around Mysuru.

Mysuru Palace

One of India's most famous palaces, Mysuru Palace is known for its magnificent architecture, royal interiors and spectacular illumination.

Chamundi Hills

Located near Mysuru, Chamundi Hills is famous for the Chamundeshwari Temple and offers beautiful panoramic views of the city.

Brindavan Gardens

Located near the Krishnarajasagara Dam, Brindavan Gardens is famous for its landscaped gardens and musical fountain.

Chamundeshwari Temple

A historic temple located on Chamundi Hill and one of the important spiritual landmarks of Mysuru.

Mysuru Zoo

Sri Chamarajendra Zoological Gardens, commonly called Mysuru Zoo, is a popular destination for wildlife and nature lovers.

St. Philomena's Church

A beautiful neo-Gothic church known for its impressive architecture and tall twin spires.
